# Bot ko AI nahi, proof chahiye

Market ke zyada tar "AI trading tools" asal mein AI se price predict nahi karte. Wo hand-written rules, pattern-recognition engines, ML ranking scores ya upar lagi LLM (ChatGPT/Claude jaisi language model) chat layer hain, aur **inme se kisi ki bhi accuracy independently audited nahi mili**; jahan LLM ne khud trade kiya, wahan nateeja kamzor ya bahut bikhra hua raha. Hamara bot levels aur setups mein TradingView, LuxAlgo, Autochartist aur Trading Central jaisa output already deta hai, aur NSE, crypto aur forex ek saath cover karta hai. Uski asli kami **proof (backtest aur forward journal), data ki sahi definitions aur derivatives/regime context** hai, AI nahi. Data timing har market mein alag hai: Binance crypto exact hai (din 05:30 IST se chalta hai), NSE stocks aur indices ek din ke test mein 1 minute se kam purane mile aur previous-day levels sahi nikle, jabki BSE aur Sensex ~15 minute late hain. Sabse badi galtiyan latency mein nahi, definitions mein nikli: Yahoo ka forex daily Close lagbhag **ek din peeche** chalta hai (EURUSD par 50 mein se 46 din Close ≈ Open), aur gold `GC=F` spot nahi balki COMEX futures hai, jo ek reading mein ~10 minute late tha aur roz ~6 ghante **ek session peeche** rehta hai (5 Nov ko bot ka pivot 3,942 tha, sahi ~4,027). Isliye precise banne ka raasta naya AI jodna nahi hai: pehle ye definitions theek karo, phir future-data leak ke bina backtest Indian costs aur random baselines ke saath chalao, aur har alert par bot ka apna measured hit-rate dikhao. In tools mein se koi ye publish nahi karta, aur personal use ke liye ye ₹0–500/mahina ke data mein ho sakta hai.

## Zyada tar "AI" tools rules, pattern-engine ya LLM chat nikle

Ye report paanch research notes, bot ke code aur 5 Nov 2026 ko bot ke apne data path (yfinance 1.8.0, ccxt) par chalaye read-only tests par based hai. Kaam ke model ke hisaab se saare tools chaar type ke hain: hand-written rules (TradingView aur LuxAlgo indicators, Chartink, Streak), statistical pattern recognition (Autochartist, Trading Central), ML ranking ya statistical selection (Danelfin, Kavout, Holly, TrendSpider ka AI Strategy Lab), aur LLM layer jo aapki baat ko inhi mechanics mein translate karti hai ya data summarise karti hai (TradingView AI Copilot, Zerodha Kite MCP, Groww GR 1). 2025–26 ka asli badlaav ye hai ki LLM ab chart aur broker ke andar baith gaya hai. Robinhood ne 27 May 2026 ko third-party agents ke liye Agentic Trading beta khola aur saaf kaha ki wo un agents ko control ya audit nahi karta ([Robinhood](https://robinhood.com/newsroom/robinhood-is-now-open-to-agents)). Hamara bot bhi levels aur setups screen par rakhta hai, isliye tulna seedhi hai.

## Kisi vendor ki accuracy audited nahi, aur LLM khud trade karke kamzor rahe

Bazaar mein jitne performance numbers ghoomte hain, sab vendor ke apne backtest ya vendor-reported hain. Danelfin ka **AI Score 10 par "+21.05% annualized alpha"** 3 Jan 2017 se 1 Apr 2025 ka backtest hai, jisme naye model ke liye purane scores dobara calculate hue aur koi audit mention nahi hai ([Danelfin](https://blog.danelfin.com/?p=4653)); us date ke baad ka live record humein nahi mila, jabki asli number wahi hota. Tickeron ke agent figures "historical backtested or platform-reported" hain ([FinancialContent](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/prlog-2026-8-12-tickeron-highlights-ai-trading-agent-performance-across-multiple-market-sectors)). TrendSpider ka 119.4% return ek vendor case study hai ([TrendSpider](https://trendspider.com/blog/building-a-strategy-with-ai-strategy-lab/)). Holly ka reviews mein ghoomta ~68% win-rate internal simulations se hai aur audited nahi, aur ye source khud affiliate-style aur kamzor hai ([AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal-56o.pages.dev/blog/trade-ideas-holly-ai-review/)). Trading Central awards dikhata hai, jo audit nahi hote ([Trading Central](https://www.tradingcentral.com/awards/trading-central-for-best-ai-solution)).

Independent evidence sirf general LLMs par hai. Academic StockBench benchmark kehta hai ki "most models struggle to outperform the simple buy-and-hold baseline" ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02209)), aur TradeTrap dikhata hai ki autonomous agents ko "systematically misled" kiya ja sakta hai ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02261v1)). Nof1 ke live Alpha Arena Season 1 (har LLM ko USD 10,000, crypto, ~18 Oct se 3–4 Nov 2025) mein Qwen 3 Max ~+22% se jeeta, jabki ek tally mein Claude Sonnet 4.5 −30.8%, Grok 4 −45.3%, Gemini 2.5 Pro −56.7% aur **GPT-5 −62.7%** par rahe ([GN Crypto](https://www.gncrypto.news/news/qwen-wins-alpha-arena-season-1-with-22-percent-returns/)). US equities wale Season 1.5 mein Grok 4.20 ~+12% se jeeta ([Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/news/ai-model-grok-4-2-triumphs-in-trading-tournament)). Nof1 ne khud season ko chhota aur statistical controls ke bina exploratory kaha, aur outlets ke figures mein thoda conflict hai, isliye ranking par bharosa mat karo. Sabak sirf ye hai ki LLM jab khud decide karta hai to results bahut bikhre hote hain.

Regulators legit tools ka audit nahi karte, wo overclaiming aur fraud pakadte hain. SEC ne March 2024 mein Delphia (USD 225,000) aur Global Predictions (USD 175,000) ke saath pehle "AI-washing" settlements kiye ([Mondaq](https://webiis08.mondaq.com/canada/new-technology/1743326/ai-washing-the-new-greenwashing-how-the-secs-emerging-technologies-unit-is-rewriting-the-compliance-landscape)), aur SEBI ke June 2025 consultation ne AI/ML use karne wali firms ke liye disclosure, accuracy reporting aur 5 saal ka record prastavit kiya, jiske final hone ki pushti nahi mili ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/sebi-proposes-ai-ml-guidelines-for-market-participants-125062001137_1.html)). Isliye kisi vendor par action na hona iska saboot nahi ki uske signals kaam karte hain. Indian base rate aur sakht hai: SEBI ke mutabik FY25 mein **91% individual F&O traders ne paisa gawaya** ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/net-losses-of-traders-in-fo-widens-in-fy25-sebi-study-125070701221_1.html)); FY26 mein 87.7%, ₹91,685 crore loss aur ~₹25,000 crore sirf transaction costs (secondary coverage) ([Open Magazine](https://openthemagazine.com/business/sebi-fo-loss-study-explained-why-9-in-10-retail-traders-lost-91685-crore-in-fy26)); cash intraday mein FY23 par 71% loss mein, 500+ trades/saal walon mein 80%, aur loss walon ke costs unke loss ke 57% ([SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/media-and-notifications/press-releases/jul-2024/sebi-study-finds-that-7-out-of-10-individual-intraday-traders-in-equity-cash-segment-make-losses_84948.html)). Koi bhi signal, AI ka ho ya hamara, tabhi kaam ka hai jab wo is prior ko apne out-of-sample (jo data tuning mein use nahi hua) aur net-of-cost numbers se kaate.

### Bot ke rules ka evidence: time-of-day aur S/R mazboot, CPR aur sweeps kamzor

Literature ke saamne bot ke components ki tasveer mili-juli hai. Technical analysis ke 95 "modern" studies mein 56 ne profit paaya, par authors ne data snooping aur costs ko badi samasya bataya ([IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jecsur/v21y2007i4p786-826.html)). Daily/weekly trend ko time-series momentum ka sahara hai, jo 58 liquid futures mein mila ([EconPapers](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:jfinec:v:104:y:2012:i:2:p:228-250)). Intraday mein pehle aadhe ghante ka return aakhri aadhe ghante ko predict karta hai, par SPY par predictive R² sirf 1.6% hai ([Alpha Architect](https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/08/attention-prop-traders-the-first-half-hour-of-trading-predicts-the-last-half-hour/)); ye effect negative dealer-gamma wale dinon mein tez hota hai ([EconPapers](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:jfinec:v:142:y:2021:i:1:p:377-403)) aur Bitcoin mein bhi mila ([Reading](https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/100181)), lekin NSE par aisa koi study nahi mila. ORB par sabse kaam ka saboot ek independent 2026 replication hai: gross edge (+0.05 se +0.13R) dobara mila, par 6–12% of risk ke spread aur slippage ke baad net expectancy NQ par **+0.002R** aur baaki chaar markets mein −0.04 se −0.08R ho gaya ([MQL5 blog](https://www.mql5.com/en/blogs/post/776235)). S/R ka sabse mazboot sahara Osler ka bank order data hai: take-profit orders round numbers par aur **stop-loss orders round numbers ke thoda paar** cluster karte hain ([NY Fed SR125](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/staff_reports/sr125.pdf)), aur stop clusters tak pahunchne par trend ghanton tak tez hota hai ([NY Fed SR150](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/staff_reports/sr150.html)). Zyada touches wale level par bounce zyada aur purane level par kam hota hai ([IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/p/arx/papers/2101.07410.html)).

SMC/ICT sweeps ka koi peer-reviewed test nahi mila. Aug 2026 ka ek company test, jiske definitions hamare bot jaise hain (5-bar fractal swings, equal highs/lows, PDH/PDL, wick ke baad close wapas), kehta hai ki EURUSD par sweep ke baad reversal 75.10% hua jabki random levels par **77.82%** (z = −21.02), sabhi 18 instrument × category z-scores negative the, aur spread ke baad net expectancy −0.10R se −0.25R thi ([eXpeed](https://www.expeed.co.jp/ict-buy-side-sell-side-liquidity-an-empirical-test/)). Ye test peer-reviewed nahi hai aur author ka naam nahi hai, par iska sabak important hai: 75% ka raw reversal rate impressive lagta hai, jabki random level bhi utna hi ya usse zyada deta hai. Grades ye hain: A = peer-reviewed aur replicated, B = single peer-reviewed study ya strong working paper, C = unreviewed paper ya transparent practitioner test, D = vendor material ya untested convention.

Teen design-level baatein bot ke logic mein seedhe dikhti hain. Pehli, **CPR width asal mein close location hai**: formula se width = (2/3)·|C − (H+L)/2| nikalta hai, yaani "narrow CPR" ka matlab sirf itna hai ki kal ka close range ke beech mein tha, chahe range kitni bhi badi ho. Bot ka "narrow → trending/breakout day likely" hint simple range percentile aur close location ke saamne test hona chahiye. Doosri, **pivots aur CPR PDH/PDL/PDC ko double-count karte hain**: P, R1, S1, R2, S2 aur CPR sab kal ke H, L, C se hi bante hain, phir bhi bot unhe alag levels ki tarah confluence mein jodta hai (`abd_bot/analysis/zones.py`), isliye "PDH + R1" jaisa zone apne asli evidence se zyada strong dikhta hai. Teesri, **bias ke votes ek doosre se correlated hain**: EMA alignment, HH/HL, RSI, CPR aur VWAP sab recent direction hi naapte hain, isliye 5 Nov subah gold ka "+5/5" aur EURUSD ka "+4/4" paanch alag saboot nahi, lagbhag ek hi saboot hai.

### NSE aur BSE: NSE feed fresh, BSE 15 minute late, aur 15:15 ke baad auction

Yahoo ka ek page version NSE real-time ke saath "signed-in users" ki shart lagata hai ([Yahoo Help](https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN2310.html)), aur bot bina sign-in ke data leta hai, isliye humne naapa. **5 Nov 2026 ko teen baar (~09:28–09:45, ~11:07 aur ~12:14 IST) `RELIANCE.NS`, `HDFCBANK.NS`, `^NSEI`, `^NSEBANK` aur `^INDIAVIX` ka latest 1-minute bar chalti hui minute ka mila (bar open se ghadi ka farak 0.3–1.0 minute), jabki `RELIANCE.BO` aur `^BSESN` 15.0–15.8 minute peeche the.** Matlab us din NSE feed near real-time tha aur BSE wala ~15 minute late; Sensex Yahoo ki table mein nahi hai, par wo bhi BSE stock ki tarah late mila. Bot ki "Yahoo data kuch minute late ho sakta hai" wali warning NSE par us din nahi dikhi. Ye sirf ek din ka test hai: isne bar ka timestamp naapa, price ko broker tick se match nahi kiya, aur auction window ko cover nahi kiya (raw output notes folder ki `nse_lag_probe_2026-11-05.txt` mein hai). Forming 1-minute bar ka volume har baar 0 tha, yaani volume price se thoda peeche aata hai.

3 Aug 2026 se F&O stocks mein continuous trading **15:15** par khatam hoti hai, phir **Closing Auction Session (CAS)** chalta hai (matching 15:30–15:35), aur official close auction ka price hota hai; non-F&O stocks ka close 15:00–15:30 ka VWAP hi hai ([TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/sebi-introduces-closing-auction-session-cas-for-f-o-stocks-and-revises-pre-open-session/190558); [Zerodha support](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/trading-faqs/articles/ltp-doesnt-match-close); [SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/jan-2026/introduction-of-closing-auction-session-cas-in-the-equity-cash-segment-and-certain-modifications-in-the-pre-open-auction-session_99122.html)). **7 Dec 2026 se** ye phir badlega: continuous 15:30 tak, CAS 15:30–15:40, derivatives 15:45 tak ([tick2trade](https://www.tick2trade.com/blogs/sebi-revises-nse-closing-auction-session-rules); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/markets/sebi-revises-closing-auction-session-rules-retains-30-minute-vwap-settlement-method)); ye timings secondary sources se hain, SEBI ka text nahi padha gaya. Index close bhi auction ke baad banta hai, aur pehle chaar CAS sessions mein 15:15 level se final close ka average gap ~0.42% tha ([Multibagg](https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/nifty-closing-price-cas-gap-cmsta63gb59h90zo5snn46x5n)).

4 Nov 2026 ke bars mein Yahoo ka tarika saaf dikha. RELIANCE ke 75 mein se 74 bars aaye: 15:10 tak normal, **15:15 ka bar lagbhag khaali (volume 19), 15:20 ka bar gayab, aur 15:25 ka flat bar ₹1,311.00 par 28.25 lakh shares ke saath**. Ye auction print hai: din ke volume ka ~14.5%, aur 15:10 ke close ₹1,315.50 se 0.34% neeche. Iska stamp asli matching se 5–10 minute **peeche** hai, isliye backtest mein ise 15:25 par tradable mat maanna. NIFTY 15:20 tak 23,911.70 par flat raha aur 15:25 ke bar mein 23,960.60 par kooda (+48.9 points, +0.20%). Din ka high bhi isi jump se bana, yaani agle din ka PDH aur buy-side pool ek auction price par baithta hai, jo test karne layak sawaal hai. Achhi baat ye hai ki **Yahoo ki daily row ka close isi auction print se match hua**, aur bot ke pre-market plan mein RELIANCE ke PDH ₹1,318.90, PDL ₹1,296.00, PDC ₹1,311.00 aur P ₹1,308.63 usi row se aaye; official bhavcopy se ise cross-check nahi kiya gaya. Intraday bars din ke extremes thoda miss karte hain: RELIANCE ka daily low ₹1,296.00 tha par 5m ka lowest ₹1,297.10, NIFTY ka 23,696.15 par 5m ka 23,700.80. Isliye live Day High/Low, ORB, swing points aur sweep ke wicks approximate hain, jabki completed din ke levels daily row se behtar hain.

Bot ke logic mein NSE ki teen timing kamiyan hain (`abd_bot/analysis/session.py`). Market close 15:30 set hai, isliye bot 15:15–15:30 mein "LIVE" dikhata hai jabki F&O stocks aur indices auction mein flat hain, aur is window ka "15m close above X" bekaar hai. Agar plan 15:30 ke baad lekin auction print aane se pehle bane, to bot 15:15 ke price ko close maan sakta hai. Pivot (H+L+C)/3 hone se close ki galti ΔC pivot ko ΔC/3 aur TC, R1, S1 ko 2ΔC/3 khiskati hai, to early-CAS ka 0.42% gap (NIFTY ~24,600 par ~103 points) P ko ~34 aur TC ko ~69 points galat kar dega. Print Yahoo mein kitni der mein aata hai, ye live nahi naapa gaya. Teesri kami holidays ki hai: bot sirf Saturday/Sunday pehchanta hai, isliye weekday holiday par "Market open" jaisa status aur holiday ki date ka plan dega. Index par bot sahi tarah VWAP nahi dikhata, aur stocks ka VWAP 5m bars se approximation hai, jabki broker feeds exchange ka average traded price seedha dete hain ([Kite docs](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/websocket/)).

### Forex: quotes real-time, par daily Close ek din peeche

Forex quotes Yahoo par fresh mile: ek reading mein `EURUSD=X` ka aakhri 1-minute bar ~1 minute purana tha. `USDINR=X` ka ~10 minute purana tha, jo us waqt thin quoting ya delay dono ho sakta hai, isliye inconclusive hai. Volume zero hai, isliye bot VWAP sahi tarah skip karta hai. Problem din ke boundary aur daily close mein hai. Bot din Yahoo ke daily index ki timezone se leta hai, jo test mein **Europe/London** nikli: din winter mein 05:30 IST aur BST mein 04:30 IST par badalta hai, jabki OANDA ka default **New York 17:00** hai aur brokers ke boundaries alag-alag hote hain ([async-v20 docs](https://async-v20.readthedocs.io/en/latest/annotations.html); [MQL5 forum](https://www.mql5.com/en/forum/159190)). Isliye bot ke PDH/PDL aapke broker chart se thode alag ho sakte hain.

Usse bada mamla ye hai ki **Yahoo ka completed-din ka FX daily Close us din ka close hai hi nahi**. Hamare test mein ~70 din ki look-back (50 poore London din) par `EURUSD=X` ka daily Close usi din ke aakhri 1-hour close se median **18.0 pips** (max 79.9) door tha, aur **50 mein se 46 din Close, Open ke ±2 pips ke andar** tha. `GBPUSD=X` par median 29.1 pips (max 100.7, 40/50 din Close ≈ Open) aur `USDJPY=X` par 29.5 pips (max 280.2, 26/50 din) mila. EURUSD par Yahoo ka Close lagbhag pichhle din ka asli close nikla (4 Nov ka Close 1.1207, 3 Nov ka aakhri price 1.1211), yaani **daily close series ek din peeche chalti hai**. High aur Low ~3 pips ke andar match hue, isliye PDH/PDL lagbhag theek hain.

5 Nov subah ke bot output mein iska seedha asar dikha. EURUSD ka PDC 1.1207 tha, jabki Yahoo ke apne 15m bars ke hisaab se 4 Nov (London din) ka aakhri price 1.1238 tha. Isliye P 1.1228 aaya (sahi ~1.1238), R1 1.1255 (sahi ~1.1275), S1 1.1180 (sahi ~1.1200), aur "1D +0.49%" asal mein ~+0.22% tha. Bot ne CPR ko "Width 0.18%, wide, sideways/range day likely" bataya, jabki sahi close ke saath CPR lagbhag ek line hota, kyunki close bilkul mid-range par tha, aur bot ka hi rule tab "narrow" kehta. Yaani galat close ne din ka read hi ulta kar diya. Swing aur long-term forex plans ke EMA/RSI bhi isi series par chalte hain.

Indian resident ke liye ye levels zyada tar analysis ke kaam ke hain. RBI ke mutabik FEMA ke tahat offshore forex trading permitted nahi aur LRS se margin nahi bheja ja sakta ([RBI FAQ](https://website.rbi.org.in/documents/d/rbi/foreign-exchange-forex-transactions)); Alert List 19 Nov 2025 tak 95 entities ki thi ([Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/unauthorised-forex-trading-platforms-rbi-adds-7-more-entities-to-its-alert-list/78900.html)). Legal raasta exchange-traded currency derivatives hain, jo formally hedging ke liye hain ([RBI](https://website.rbi.org.in/web/rbi/-/notifications/risk-management-and-inter-bank-dealings-revised-guidelines-relating-to-participation-of-a-person-resident-in-india-and-foreign-portfolio-investor-fpi-in-the-exchange-traded-currency-derivatives-etcd-market-11222)), aur unka price aur session `USDINR=X` se alag hai. Public data se apne liye levels nikalna regulated act nahi lagta; exposure wahan hai jahan trade execute aur fund hota hai (hamari reading, legal advice nahi).

### Gold, silver, crude: delayed futures, aur ~6 ghante ek session peeche

Bot `GOLD`/`XAUUSD` ko COMEX futures `GC=F` par, silver ko `SI=F` aur crude ko `CL=F` par map karta hai. Yahoo ki table COMEX aur NYMEX ko 30 minute delayed batati hai; hamari ek reading mein `GC=F` aur `CL=F` ka aakhri 1-minute bar ~10 minute aur `SI=F` ka ~11 minute purana tha. Dono soorat mein "15m close" confirmation kam se kam ~10 minute late aata hai, aur table sahi ho to ~30 minute. Futures aur spot ka farak (basis) bhi hota hai: Aug 2025 mein ek hafte ke andar ye ~USD 40 se **USD 100/oz ke upar** chala gaya tha ([Saxo](https://www.home.saxo/pl-pl/content/articles/commodities/tariff-shock-sends-gold-futures-soaring--yet-spot-market-holds-the-real-signal-08082025)). Nov 2026 ka basis notes mein nahi mila. Continuous `=F` series mein contract roll ke jumps alag aa sakte hain, aur GC roz 17:00–18:00 ET band rehta hai ([CME](https://cmegroup.com/trading/metals/precious/gold_contractSpecs_futures)), jo test mein 16:45 aur 18:00 ET ke beech bars ke gap se dikha.

Sabse badi galti din ke boundary ki hai. Test mein Yahoo ki `GC=F` daily rows **CME session** (pichhle din 18:00 ET se 17:00 ET) follow karti mili: 29 Oct, 30 Oct aur 3 Nov ki rows ke O/H/L 15m session data se exact match hue, aur Monday 2 Nov ki row thodi alag thi. Bot "aaj" ko **New York calendar din** (00:00–24:00 ET) maanta hai. Nateeja: roz 18:00 ET se NY midnight tak (winter mein ~04:30–10:30 IST, summer mein ~03:30–09:30 IST) bot ka gold "previous day" **ek session purana** rehta hai, aur din bhar "today" ka open, high, low aur VWAP session se match nahi karte.

5 Nov ko 08:45 IST ke bar par bot ne gold ka PDC 3,940.4 (3 Nov session) liya, jabki pichhla session (4 Nov, 03:30 IST par band) Yahoo ke 15m bars mein 4,072.2 par close hua tha. Isliye "1D +5.21%" dikha jabki sahi ~+1.8% tha. Bot ka P 3,942.07 aaya, jabki 4 Nov session (H 4,081.2, L 3,928.2, C 4,072.2) se P ~4,027 aur CPR ~4,005–4,050 banta. Bot ke saare pivots (S2 3,864 se R2 4,020) price ~4,146 se neeche the, yaani us waqt bekaar. Upar se Yahoo ki chalti hui 4 Nov daily row khud kharab thi: open 3,940.8 purane session ka, par high 4,149.1, low 4,067.0 aur close ~4,143 naye session ke, yaani low open se upar. NY midnight ke baad Yahoo ise theek karta hai ya nahi, ye verify nahi hua; na kare to PDH/PDL bhi galat aa sakte hain. India mein exchange-traded raasta MCX gold (INR) hai, jo alag instrument aur session hai; iske rules yahan research nahi hue.

In sab tests ki limits saaf hain. NSE/BSE wala test ek din ke teen snapshots hain, aur baaki 4–5 Nov ke data par ek-ek run hain, jinka raw output save nahi hua; yfinance ke 15m, 1h aur 1d bars compare karke inhe dobara kiya ja sakta hai. Tulna Yahoo ke apne intraday bars se hai, broker ya bhavcopy se nahi. Yahoo in behaviours ko document nahi karta aur ye kabhi bhi badal sakte hain.

### Proof engine: future data ka leak band, costs poore

Sabse pehla niyam: har feature sirf wahi data use kare jo decision ke waqt maujood tha. Swing high tabhi "exist" karta hai jab uske baad ke k bars band ho jaayein. Bot ka k = 3 hai, yaani 5m chart par 15 minute aur 15m chart par 45 minute baad. Chart par ye swing peeche draw hota hai, isi wajah se history live se behtar dikhti hai, aur TradingView aur LuxAlgo dono isi "repainting" ki chetavni dete hain ([Pine docs](https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/concepts/repainting/); [LuxAlgo](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/repaint-safe-engineering.md)). Check simple hai: kisi bhi random time par data ko wahin kaat kar saare levels dobara nikalo. Full-history run se ek bhi value alag aayi to wo future data ka leak hai.

Phir har trigger ko ek event maano aur dekho pehle kya aaya: target, stop ya time-out (triple-barrier labeling) ([AFML contents](https://toc.uni.li/FLMF049641.pdf)). Costs poore lagao: Zerodha schedule par futures STT 0.05% aur options premium par 0.15% hai, jo 1 Apr 2026 se badha ([Zerodha charges](https://zerodha.com/charges); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/stocks/story/zerodha-doubling-fees-for-select-intraday-trades-who-will-be-impacted-522295-2026-03-25)). Is hisaab se ₹10 lakh notional ka index-futures round trip ~₹612.8 (6.1 bp) aur ₹1 lakh ka equity intraday round trip ~₹82.7 (8.3 bp) padta hai, slippage alag. 0.30% ke futures stop par ye cost ~0.20R hai, aur breakeven hit-rate (1 + c)/(1 + R) se c = 0.2R par 1R target ko **60%**, 1.5R ko 48% aur 2R ko 40% chahiye. Bot abhi sirf ≥1R target maangta hai, aur 5 Nov ke outputs mein 1:1.2 se 1:1.4 wale setups the, isliye minimum target 1.5–2R karke test karo aur jin setups ka stop round-trip cost ke 2–3 guna se chhota ho unhe hatao. Binance fees, funding aur FX spreads abhi research nahi hue, isliye unhe apne fills se naapna hoga; India mein spot crypto par 30% tax aur 1% TDS bhi net result badalte hain (secondary source, verify karna) ([TDSMAN](https://blog.tdsman.com/2026/05/tds-on-virtual-digital-assets-section-3931-194s/)).

"Generic tools se behtar" ka matlab measurable hona chahiye. Har setup ko paanch baselines se net-of-cost harana hoga: same time aur same stop/target wale random entries, same doori par random levels, sirf PDH/PDL/PDC, generic floor pivots, aur pehle candle ki direction vs random direction. Phir ek-ek level type aur har bias vote hata kar dekho ki result girta hai ya nahi; umeed hai ki pivots aur CPR, PDH/PDL/PDC mein merge ho jaayenge. Sweep ko eXpeed jaise random-level test ke saamne rakho, reversal aur tez continuation dono ke liye. Pehle wahi hypotheses test karo jinke peeche literature hai: NIFTY/BANKNIFTY ka pehla vs aakhri aadha ghanta (CAS ke hisaab se window badal kar), ORB volatility aur relative volume ke saath, S/R bounce vs touch count aur age, aur BTC ka 1–4 ghante ka mean reversion ([LMU](https://epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de/97651/)).

Results sirf out-of-sample data par jaancho, kyunki Bitcoin par ~15,000 rules ka in-sample edge out-of-sample gayab ho gaya tha ([Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10479-019-03357-1)). Walk-forward mein jaise 12 mahine par tune karo aur agle 3 mahine par test, aur 3 Aug 2026 ke baad ka CAS period locked holdout rakho, yaani aisa data jise tuning khatam hone tak chhoona hi nahi. NSE history ko structural breaks par todo: Nov 2024 (SEBI F&O measures) ([Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/business-updates/sebis-new-rules-for-index-derivatives-heres-whats-changing)), 1 Sep 2025 (Tuesday expiry) ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/nse-bids-adieu-to-thursday-expiry-as-dates-swap-come-into-effect-explained-125082800635_1.html)), 1 Apr 2026 (STT), 3 Aug 2026 (CAS) aur 7 Dec 2026 (naye timings). Har try kiya config log karo: jitne zyada try, utna luck ka chance. Isliye Deflated Sharpe Ratio ([SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2460551)) aur Probability of Backtest Overfitting ([SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2326253)) report karo, aur naye filter ke liye t-statistic ~3 ka hurdle rakho ([NBER](https://nber.org/papers/w20592)). Data ke liye Yahoo kaafi nahi: Dhan 5 saal ka intraday deta hai par expired futures nahi ([Dhan](https://dhan.co/support/platforms/dhanhq-api/how-can-i-retrieve-data-for-expired-futures-contracts-using-the-apis/)), aur crypto ke liye Binance public API free hai.

### Bharose ki layer: bot apna hit-rate khud dikhaye

Abhi confluence weights (PDH 3, CPR 2–3, R1 2, S/R 1) aur bias votes haath se set hain. Inki jagah ek simple model (regularised logistic regression) walk-forward fit karo jo bataye ki is tarah ke setup mein target stop se pehle aane ki probability kitni hai; inputs level type, touch count, level age, ATR mein doori, time of day aur regime flags hon. Phir check karo ki jin alerts par bot ne 58% kaha, wo asal mein ~58% baar sahi nikle ya nahi (calibration). Tab har alert par "is tarah ke setups ka hit-rate X% ± Y" jaisa number dikh sakta hai, jise aap baad mein realised result se mila sako.

Saath hi shadow mode chalao: Telegram alert ke saath ek append-only journal, jisme alert ke waqt timestamp, setup, level type, confluence, votes, regime, planned entry/SL/target, cost aur bot version likha ho, aur baad mein fill, slippage, outcome aur net R jude. Row hash karo taaki history baad mein badal na sake. Sample size ka dhyan rakho: 50% ke aas-paas hit-rate par 95% interval n = 100 par ±9.8 points, n = 400 par ±4.9 aur n = 1,000 par ±3.1 hota hai, aur 55% ko 50% se alag karne ke liye har group mein ~1,565 events chahiye. Isliye kuch hafton ka paper trading zyada tar noise hai; estimate backtest se karo aur confirm forward test se (kam se kam ~3 mahine ya 300+ alerts per asset class). Gradient boosting jaisa ML tabhi jab hazaron labelled events hon, aur wo sirf rule-candidates ko filter ya size kare, khud trade generate na kare. Ye thresholds hamari reasoning hain, kisi study se nahi.

### Naya data, risk rules aur kya kharidna hai

Naya data evidence ke order mein jodo. Sabse pehle calendar flags: NIFTY weekly expiry Tuesday, Sensex Thursday, RBI, Budget aur results days, aur US CPI/NFP/FOMC, kyunki announcement surprises FX mein turant jump laate hain ([NBER](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w8959/w8959.pdf)) aur India mein index-options turnover ka 59% expiry day par hota hai. Phir India VIX ya ATR percentile se volatility state, kyunki ORB returns volatility regime par kaafi depend karte hain (search summary se, full text verify nahi) ([Umeå](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:hhs:umnees:0948)). Single stocks ke liye relative volume ([Zarattini et al.](https://ideas.repec.org/p/chf/rpseri/rp2498.html)). NIFTY ke max-OI strikes experimental levels hain: dealer gamma ka US mein mazboot evidence hai, India mein nahi mila. Crypto mein Binance klines ke taker-buy volume se CVD (buy aur sell taker volume ka cumulative farak) free mein ban sakta hai, par uske liye raw kline call chahiye kyunki bot abhi sirf OHLCV rakhta hai; liquidation feed undercount karta hai, ye yaad rakhna. FII/DII (session ke baad, provisional) ([NSE](https://www.nseindia.com/reports/fii-dii)) aur GIFT Nifty (sirf gap estimate, rigorous study nahi mili) sabse neeche hain.

Liquidity ko "stop-cluster hypothesis" ki tarah rakho. Osler ka evidence stops ko round numbers ke thoda paar dikhata hai, swing points ke paar nahi; bot round levels already jodta hai, ab unhe swing pools ke saamne test karna hai. Forex mein OANDA Order Book stop clusters se cross-check de sakta hai, aur crypto mein wick ke saath liquidation burst aur OI drop sweep ko confirm kar sakte hain; ye untested hypothesis hai. Test hone tak sweep vote ko bias score se bahar rakho aur CPR width hint par "unvalidated" label lagao.

Risk rules signal jitne hi zaroori hain, aur evidence zyada tar behavioural hai. Subah haarne wale CBOT traders ke dopahar mein above-average risk lene ke chances ~15–16% zyada the ([IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jfinan/v60y2005i1p1-34.html)), stop-loss trend wale returns mein value jodte hain par random walk mein ghatate hain ([EconPapers](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:finmar:v:18:y:2014:i:c:p:234-254)), aur volatility targeting extreme returns ghatata hai ([Quantpedia](https://quantpedia.com/the-impact-of-volatility-targeting-on-equities-bonds-commodities-and-currencies)). Bot ke risk layer ke example defaults ye ho sakte hain: stop distance se size, 0.25–0.5% risk per trade, −2R par din band, 3 lagaataar losses par pause, aur trades-per-day cap. Ye thresholds judgement hain, evidence nahi, aur inhe aapko apne hisaab se tay karna hai. 40% hit-rate par 250 trades mein ~9–10 aur 20% par ~17–20 ki losing streak expected hai, isliye size aisa ho ki wo streak jheli ja sake.

Kharidne ke sawaal par: Trade Ideas, Danelfin, Kavout aur Tickeron par personal use ke liye paisa lagane ka reason nahi mila, kyunki NSE coverage nahi ya confirm nahi, aur unka edge audited nahi. Liquidation heatmap bhi zaroori nahi, wo estimate hai. Kaam ki cheezein sasti hain: apne broker ka data API, TradingView cross-check chart ki tarah (NSE real-time ke liye broker link), Zerodha par ho to Sensibull aur Streak free, aur Chartink Premium tab jab poore NSE ka scan chahiye. LLM ko sirf narrator rakho jo bot ke compute kiye numbers samjhaye; usse level mat poochho.

Compliance par hamari reading ye hai (legal advice nahi): personal use mein bot RA registration ke daayre mein nahi aata, kyunki RA definition "for consideration" research services par lagti hai ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/securities/1574012/navigating-the-updated-regulatory-framework-for-investment-advisers-and-research-analysts)). Agar chhota group bhi dekhe, to usme paisa ya koi indirect fayda na ho, aur yaad rahe ki Yahoo redistribution mana karta hai. Bot khud order bheje tab 1 Apr 2026 ke rules lagte hain: static IP, roz 2FA aur 10 orders/second tak generic algo ID ([Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/general/a-comprehensive-overview-of-nses-circular-on-the-new-retail-algo-trading-framework); [FYERS](https://fyers.in/notice-board/new-sebi-framework-for-retail-algo-trading-from-april-01-2026)). Ye report research aur engineering ke liye hai, investment advice nahi.

## Conclusion

Is research ke baad sawaal badal jaata hai: "kaunsa AI tool lein" se "hamare numbers kitne sach hain". 2026 mein AI zyada tar ek interface hai, edge nahi, aur iska ek khatarnak pehlu hai. Agar aaj bot par LLM narrator laga dein, to wo EURUSD ke galat CPR aur gold ke purane pivots ko bhi poore confidence se samjhayega, aur galti zyada convincing ho jaayegi. Isi tarah galat close par chala backtest galat levels ko "proven" bata dega. Isliye definitions ka fix sirf ek bug fix nahi hai; ye baaki har cheez ki shart hai, aur iski keemat lagbhag zero hai.

Doosri baat ye hai ki backtest ka imaandaar jawab "koi setup costs ke baad nahi bacha" bhi ho sakta hai; ORB replication aur sweep test isi disha mein ishaara karte hain, aur SEBI ke 87–91% loss numbers bhi. Tab bot ki value signal generator ki nahi, balki sahi level map, regime warnings aur discipline tool ki hogi, aur ye bhi ek kaam ka nateeja hai. Personal bot ka asli fayda yahin hai: vendor ko feature bechna hota hai, isliye wo kamzor rule hata nahi sakta, jabki hum jo rule test mein fail ho use agle din delete kar sakte hain. Agar aage ke din NSE par lag dikhe, ya bhavcopy cross-check mein Yahoo ke closes alag niklein, to broker feed ki priority turant upar aa jaayegi; isliye freshness label aur roz ka Yahoo vs broker diff in faislon ko chalta rakhne wale sabse saste instruments hain.
